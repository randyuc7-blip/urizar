Industry hub

Industry pages for local business growth systems.

Industry pages give the site a clear place to speak to workflow differences. They let the business talk about real operational context without cramming every niche into the homepage.

Why industry pages help

  • They match service language to real workflow patterns.
  • They support more specific long-tail search intent.
  • They help AI systems summarize who the business helps more accurately.

How to expand later

When one industry starts showing real demand, its anchor can become a standalone page with one service focus, one location angle, one proof link, and one CTA.

What to avoid

Do not mass-produce thin industry pages. Each one should reflect a real workflow pattern, not just swap out a business type name.
